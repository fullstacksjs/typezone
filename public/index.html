TypeZone

Mine gold. Send mobs. Defend your base. All by typing.

How to play
  • Tab switches between Gather and Fight mode.
  • Gather: type ore words to mine gold. Clean streaks build a combo multiplier; golden ore pays 3x but crumbles fast.
  • Fight: type the words above incoming mobs to destroy them before they reach your base. Kills pay a bounty.
  • 1–4 send mobs, 5–8 cast spells and upgrades. Enter fires Lightning when charged.
  • Esc / Backspace drops your current target. Typos break your combo!
  • Keep an eye on what your opponent is doing. Attack while they're busy mining.
  • At 2:30 overtime kicks in (faster mobs, double income). At 5:00 sudden death crumbles both bases.
